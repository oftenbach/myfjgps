# Privacy Policy for my FJ GPS

Last updated: October 2, 2026

my FJ GPS is an independent planning app for F-1 and J-1 students, developed by Torrey S. Waung.

This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app’s calculators, document-date tracker, Calendar reminders, I-94 tools, and external resources.

Privacy questions and requests may be directed to [torreywaung@icloud.com](mailto:torreywaung@icloud.com).

## Accounts, advertising, and local processing

No my FJ GPS account is required. The app does not include advertising or third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

The local calculators and document-checking features do not upload your entries or document contents to the developer or an AI service. Network-connected features, including the news feed and CBP website, are described separately below.

## Calculators and informational simulations

Dates, training periods, selections, and calculated results are processed in the current page’s memory. They are not submitted to a developer database. Leaving or reloading the page discards its working entries, although device or browser restoration behavior may vary.

The app may use session storage to remember that you dismissed an informational notice. That preference does not contain immigration-document information.

## Document scanning and saved dates

With your camera permission, the End Date Tracker uses Apple’s on-device text recognition to identify dates on an I-20, DS-2019, or EAD.

This local scanning feature processes camera frames and recognized text temporarily in memory. It does not save photographs or upload the images or recognized text. You must confirm the identified date before saving it.

When you save a date, the app stores:

- Your selected F-1 or J-1 category and document type.
- The confirmed document end date.
- Your selected reminder lead time.
- Identifiers and settings used to associate the saved record with its Calendar reminder.

It does not save the full document or its recognized text. The saved tracker file uses iOS file protection and is marked for exclusion from device backups. It remains until you replace or remove it, or delete the app and its local data.

The local scanner is separate from document capture offered through CBP’s website.

## Calendar access

Calendar access is optional. You may save a tracked date without granting Calendar permission.

The app requests access to create a reminder and find or update the reminder associated with your saved date. On supported systems, this permission is described as full Calendar access. The app’s reminder workflow does not upload your calendar contents to the developer.

You review the event in Apple’s Calendar editor before saving it. The event may contain your document end date and planning information.

Calendar events may sync through iCloud or another calendar provider. They may be visible to others if you select a shared calendar. Notification previews may also display event information, depending on your settings.

Removing a saved date from my FJ GPS does not delete an existing Calendar event. Remove that event separately in Calendar if you no longer need it.

## CBP’s I-94 and travel-history services

The I-94 and travel-history features open the official U.S. Customs and Border Protection website. You complete CBP’s consent, document capture, review, and search steps yourself.

Information entered or captured there, including passport information, is handled by CBP and any services its website uses. CBP’s document capture is not the app’s local End Date Tracker scanner.

my FJ GPS does not inject scripts into CBP’s page to read or populate passport fields, and does not copy those fields into a developer database.

The dedicated CBP web view uses a nonpersistent website data store. This reduces persistent local browser storage; it does not prevent CBP from receiving, monitoring, logging, or retaining information. If you open CBP in an external browser, that browser’s storage and privacy settings apply.

Review the notices on [CBP’s I-94 website](https://i94.cbp.dhs.gov/) and [CBP’s privacy policy](https://www.cbp.gov/site-policy-notices/privacy-policy) before submitting information.

The developer cannot delete information held by CBP. my FJ GPS is not affiliated with or endorsed by CBP or another government agency.

## Imported and exported PDFs

When you select an existing I-94 PDF, the app reads its text on your device to identify the admission class and Admit Until field. Other text in the PDF may be read during that extraction. You are asked to confirm the detected information.

The checking feature does not upload the PDF, save a separate imported copy, or persist the extracted fields as an app record. Your original file remains in the location you selected, including any cloud file provider you use.

When you choose to save the displayed CBP page as a PDF, the app creates a temporary, file-protected export file. It attempts to remove that temporary file after export or cancellation and when the CBP view is released. An interrupted session may leave temporary data until cleanup occurs; immediate secure erasure is not guaranteed.

Copies saved in Files or sent to another app remain under your control and the destination provider’s policies. They may sync or be backed up. Deleting my FJ GPS does not remove those copies.

## News requests and external websites

Pages displaying the news feed automatically request headlines when opened, approximately every six hours while open, and when you select Refresh.

In the native app, these requests currently go to the app’s hosted news endpoint at:

https://status-compass-students.torreywang.chatgpt.site/api/news

These requests do not include calculator answers, scanned document contents, or saved tracker dates. The receiving service and its hosting infrastructure nevertheless receive ordinary connection information, such as your IP address, request time, requested address, and browser or device request headers.

Hosting providers may maintain technical logs under their own policies. Their retention periods are not controlled by the app.

Opening a source link connects you to that website. External sites may use cookies, analytics, or other services and receive information you submit. This policy does not promise that external websites are free of tracking.

Support and privacy pages hosted on GitHub Pages are also subject to [GitHub’s privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

Apple may separately process App Store usage information or diagnostics under its policies and your settings, and may make reports available to the developer through App Store Connect.

## Support correspondence

If you contact the developer, your email address, message, and attachments are received through email services, including iCloud Mail. They are used to respond to your request, investigate reported issues, and maintain relevant support correspondence.

Please do not send passports, immigration documents, SEVIS IDs, I-94 numbers, birth dates, or other sensitive personal information. Use fictional dates and redacted screenshots when illustrating an issue.

Support correspondence is retained as needed to resolve and follow up on the request, unless a longer period is required for a legal obligation or dispute. You may request deletion by emailing torreywaung@icloud.com. Email-provider backups may follow separate retention schedules.

## Your choices and deletion

You may:

- Select **Remove saved date** in the End Date Tracker to delete its saved record.
- Delete Calendar reminders separately in Calendar.
- Revoke Camera or Calendar permission in your device’s Settings.
- Delete exported PDFs through Files or the destination app, including cloud copies and recently deleted items where applicable.
- Leave or reload a calculator page to discard its working entries.
- Delete the app, rather than merely offloading it, to remove its local app data.
- Request deletion of support correspondence by contacting the developer.

Revoking a permission does not delete previously saved records, Calendar events, or exported files.

The developer cannot retrieve or remotely erase information stored only on your device. Contact CBP or another service provider directly about information they hold.

The developer does not sell personal information or use the app’s local document data for advertising.

## Security

The app uses Apple-provided security features and HTTPS for its configured external connections. No device, network, or storage method can guarantee absolute security.

Protect access to your device and consider who can access a calendar, cloud folder, or other destination before saving immigration-related information there.
