# my FJ GPS Support

my FJ GPS provides planning tools for F-1 and J-1 students, including document-date reminders, OPT and academic training calculators, and access to I-94 resources.

## Contact

For technical assistance, accessibility concerns, feedback, or suspected calculation errors, email:

[torreywaung@icloud.com](mailto:torreywaung@icloud.com)

Please include:
- Your device model and iOS or iPadOS version.
- The app version.
- The tool or screen involved.
- What you expected to happen and what occurred instead.
- Steps that reproduce the issue, if possible.

You may attach a screenshot, but make sure to first redact or otherwise obscure any personally identifiable information.

**Do not send passport or immigration-document images, passport numbers, SEVIS IDs, I-94 numbers, dates of birth, or other sensitive personal information.** 

## Common questions

### A scanned date is incorrect
Compare the detected date with your document and correct it before saving. You can also enter the date manually.

### A Calendar reminder is missing
Confirm that you completed the Calendar save step and that the selected calendar is visible. Check your device’s Calendar notification permissions and Focus settings.

### An I-94 search fails
Retrieval is provided by the official U.S. Customs and Border Protection website. Try searching directly at https://i94.cbp.dhs.gov and consult CBP’s FAQs if the problem persists.

### A calculation differs from my school’s guidance
The calculators provide planning estimates based on your entries and any assumptions noted. Ask your DSO or RO/ARO to confirm the allowance and counting method applicable to you to maximize this tool's utility.

## Scope of support

Support addresses the app’s operation and content. It does not provide individualized immigration advice, authorize employment, extend immigration status, or correct government records.

For questions about your status, travel, employment authorization, or filing deadlines, contact your DSO, RO/ARO, or a qualified immigration attorney.
